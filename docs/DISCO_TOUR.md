# Disco 3.3.1 tour

A touch interface for the round Snowsky Disc. The stock player still plays the audio; Disco handles browsing, controls and its built-in apps. Exact menus are in [Settings](DISPLAY_SETTINGS.md).

## Getting around

Each theme has its own navigation: Disco's edge shard and navigation circle, Null's side menu, Squash's launcher, Zune's main menu, Braun 2.0's Home keys. Swipe from the left edge to go back. Pull down from the top for Quick Settings; with the QS style *Shade*, the first pull shows what's playing.

## Make it yours

Disco, Null, Squash and Zune each have a [guide](README.md). **Braun 2.0** (introduced in 3.3.0) is a light and dark theme in the spirit of Dieter Rams: a wall-clock Home, a radio-dial seek scale and shaded keys with orange icons. Ring, Braun and Stone remain available. Light and dark appearances, accents, fonts and shortcuts are under Settings › Display.

## Music and Immersive

Artwork, the track and its controls, laid out per theme. Tap the title for the track menu: Favourite, Lyrics, Album, Artist, View queue, Info, +Queue, +Playlist. [Immersive](IMMERSIVE_CONTROLS.md) shows a spinning record, CD or cover with lyrics.

## Library

Songs, artists, albums, genres, favourites, history, playlists and folders. Albums as a list, a wall or Coverflow; lists can scroll as a Wheel. Jump with the A–Z keys or the letter wheel. Search keeps your recent searches.

## Queue and Up Next

**Queue** is what you add to play next; **Up Next** is what the player plays after the current song.

## Sound and connections

Equalizer presets and your own profiles, gain, filter, ReplayGain, balance and volume limit. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth screens; MA Sendspin is experimental.

## Power

Screensaver, screen-off and idle power-off are in Battery & power; idle power-off never happens while playing or charging. The Sleep timer pauses or powers off.
