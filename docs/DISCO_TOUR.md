# Disco 3.4.2 tour

Touch UI for the round Snowsky Disc. FiiO's stock player handles audio; Disco handles browsing, controls and built-in
apps. Full menus: [Settings](DISPLAY_SETTINGS.md).

## Navigation

Per theme: Disco's edge shard and navigation circle, Null's side menu, Squash's launcher, Zune's main menu, Braun 2.0's
Home keys. Swipe from the left edge for Back; pull down from the top for Quick Settings (with QS style *Shade*, the
first pull shows now playing).

## Themes

Guides for Disco, Null, Squash and Zune are in the [docs index](README.md). **Braun 2.0** (3.3.0) is a Rams-inspired
light/dark theme: wall-clock Home, radio-dial seek scale, shaded keys with orange icons. Ring, Braun and Stone remain.
Appearance, accents, fonts and shortcuts: **Settings › Display**.

## Music and Immersive

Tap the title for the track menu: Favourite, Lyrics, Album, Artist, View queue, Info, +Queue, +Playlist.
[Immersive](IMMERSIVE_CONTROLS.md) shows a spinning record, CD or cover with lyrics.

## Library

Songs, artists, albums, genres, favourites, history, playlists, folders. Albums as List, Wall or Coverflow; lists can
scroll as a Wheel. Jump via A–Z keys or the letter wheel. Search remembers recent queries.

**Queue** is what you add to play next; **Up Next** is what the player plays after the current song.

## Sound and connectivity

EQ presets and custom profiles, gain, filter, ReplayGain, balance, volume limit. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth screens; each
headphone gets its best codec, including aptX / aptX HD ([experimental](BLUETOOTH.md)). Song Info shows the active
codec. MA Sendspin is experimental.

## Power

Screensaver, screen-off and idle power-off are in **Battery & power**; idle power-off never triggers while playing or
charging. The Sleep timer pauses or powers off.
