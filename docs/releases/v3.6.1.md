# Disco! 3.6.1

A new theme: Graphite.

## Graphite

A monochrome LCD look for the Disc: a title bar with play state and battery over a single rule, one fixed menu
column, and a selected row that inverts to ink with backlight-coloured text. Everything is set in ChicagoFLF, a
public-domain revival of a classic bitmap system font.

- **Backlight:** in Light, pick a **White**, **Grey** or **Amber** backlight. Dark is always ink on black.
- **Spectrum bars:** optional decorative bars on Now Playing; with them Off, the track's format is shown instead.
- **Outdoor** sits right under Light / dark. The Disco colour options are hidden, since Graphite has one ink.
- Graphite has its own Quick Settings selection, and the library, queue, search, EQ, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and the other
  screens follow its palette.
- Text in other scripts falls back to the shared international and CJK fonts.

Choose it in **Settings › Display › Theme**.
