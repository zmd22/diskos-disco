# diskOS Disco! 1.3.1

## A little bigger. A lot nicer to use.

The album cover is still the interface. This release gives the things you touch and read more
room: confident buttons, clearer type, an album back sleeve and a queue that gets out of your way.
Built on 1.3.0, with its left-handed layout, scroll styles and signed SD updates intact.

### The polish

- **Song Info becomes an album back sleeve.** A cover stamp, title and artist above a readable
  metadata card. Tap the title on Music, then **Info** (the circled i). Immersive remains available
  by holding the title with Title Hold set to Immersive, or through a shortcut.
- **A cleaner Queue.** Bigger Play, icon-only Random and Clear controls. The current song stays
  highlighted above a separator; upcoming songs follow without tiny explanatory text or duplicate
  headings. Empty controls dim and disable; Random needs at least two queued songs. Clear still
  requires two taps, and dragging to reorder still works.
- **Action menus you can read.** Track, album, artist, genre and file/folder menus get 56 px
  targets, 24 px icons and 16 px captions. Music's menu uses 70 px circles and 18 px captions.
  Confirmation controls are larger too. The compact **Queue** action still means Add to queue.
- **Bigger Shortcuts.** 50 px rows, 20 px labels and wider pills. All apps uses the same treatment;
  long names stay on one line. Pressed Quick Settings, Modes and shortcut buttons show a clear
  accent border and raised fill.
- **Two new Music fonts.** Settings > Display > Disco Options > Music Screen > **Track Font**:
  Original, **Inter Semibold**, or **Nunito Bold**. Alternatives use 30 px titles and 22 px artists,
  with international and CJK fallbacks. Applying uses the existing UI restart; music keeps playing.
- **An EQ with breathing room.** Ten bands span the screen, with larger labels, a bigger preset
  pill and **Choose a band**. The **<** control beside the preset returns from the editor.
- **Find your place again.** Library remembers the last twelve views' positions during the UI
  session. Long lists briefly show position/count; long queues reuse their summary for this.
  Highlighted long titles reveal briefly, then settle back to ellipsis.

### Small cost, better feel

Fonts are pre-rendered bitmaps: no runtime TTF loading or synthetic bold. Scroll memory lives
in RAM, with no flash writes while browsing. Feedback uses existing widgets; list position
reuses the existing timer and queue position uses a short, delayed fade. No continuous idle
animation was added. Battery consumption has not been measured separately.

### Install

Download **mq_ui** and **SHA256SUMS**, verify the binary, and sign it with the same owner keys
trusted by your Disc. Copy the complete **diskos-update** folder to the SD root, eject safely,
then Settings > System > Maintenance > **Update from SD Card**. About should show **1.3.1**.
Let the trial run for at least three minutes and accept it before restarting.
See [the complete update guide](../UPDATING.md). UI updates do not reinstall Album Roulette.

### Validation

The owner tested the polished3 UI on the Disc and approved it for release. The final 1.3.1
version rebuild is checked for the static MIPS32r2 / FP64 / NaN2008 ABI, embedded version,
theme and punctuation gates. LVGL renders cover dark/right and light/left layouts, fonts,
action menus, Queue and Shortcuts; interaction checks cover Info navigation, queue confirmation,
empty controls, scrolling and font fallbacks. The separate Album Roulette app remains 1.3.1.
