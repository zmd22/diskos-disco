# diskOS Disco! 1.2.0

**Let chance choose your next album, and enjoy smoother scrolling across the UI.**

## Album Roulette

**Too much music, nothing to play? Let the Disc surprise you.** Album Roulette turns your local collection into
an album draw: circular covers race right to left, slow down and settle on a winner. Tap the white **Play!** ball
or the cover to start that album in sequential order, with a zoom-and-fade transition into Music. Long-press the
winning cover to go back and spin again.

It is a playful way to rediscover the records you own but rarely think to choose. You get the anticipation of
the reveal, the freedom to try again, and a whole album to listen to once you accept the result. The current app
skips entries with three tracks or fewer, helping keep tiny or incomplete imports out of the draw.

In 1.2.0, a vinyl icon brings Album Roulette into the configurable Disco main menu. The main menu, Apps and
shortcuts all open a theme-aware landing page. Tap **I feel lucky!**, then **Surprise me** inside the app to spin.
Nothing starts playing until you accept the winner.

Add the entry under **Settings > Display > Disco Menu**. Install Album Roulette separately at
`/usr/data/apps/album-roulette/app`; this firmware release includes the integration, not the standalone app.
[Read the full walkthrough](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/blob/main/docs/DISCO_TOUR.md#album-roulette).

## Smoother scrolling

The 10 px drag threshold matches Disc Shelf's lighter touch response. Library and shared curved lists, including
Settings and its submenus, now resize continuously as they move. Large-list virtualization, A-Z jumps, rim
scrolling and the existing momentum settings remain intact. Straight-row themes keep their layout.

The navigation drawer uses a quick position-only animation and continues from its current position when reversed.
No blur, scaling or perpetual animation was added.

## More comfortable controls

- Quick Settings Wi-Fi/Bluetooth pills move 4 px upward.
- Working mode rows grow to 52 px, with 20 px labels, 22 px icons and 18 px status glyphs. All five stay in view.
- Settings > System > About identifies this build as **Disco! 1.2.0**, based on diskOS 1.2.4.

## Downloads and installation

`mq_ui` is the same ready-built binary committed as `payload/mq_ui`. `SHA256SUMS` and `MD5SUMS` are generated by the
existing release workflow. GitHub's source archives contain the firmware source, installer and compiled payload.
Use the existing [installation guide](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/blob/v1.2.0/docs/HOWTO.md) or
[update guide](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/blob/v1.2.0/docs/UPDATING.md). Signed SD-card update packages
still require your own signing keys; this binary is not itself a signed update package.

## Validation

Static MIPS32r2 FP64 NaN2008 compilation and the production theme gate passed. Landing previews use actual LVGL
rendering. The final 1.2.0 binary has not been physically tested here. Existing V2.57 compatibility scope is unchanged.
