# diskOS Disco! 1.3.0

A round-screen interface for the FiiO Snowsky Disc. 1.3.0 makes Disco yours to hold the way you like:
a left-handed layout, three ways for lists to scroll, tidier Settings and a proper welcome when nothing is
playing.

## New

- **Left-handed mode.** Settings › Display › Disco Options › Layout › **Side**: pick Left and tap **Apply**.
  The navigation circle moves to the left rim and everything arranged around it follows: Quick Settings,
  Weather, Working mode, Shortcuts, the Equalizer, Battery, list edges and the scroll dots. The back swipe
  starts at the right edge. The progress arc still runs clockwise, from just above the circle to just below it.
- **Scroll styles.** Disco Options › Layout › **Scroll**: *Classic* (rows follow the screen's edge),
  *Straight* (flat rows) or *Disco* (rows swing around the navigation circle like light on a CD; times stay in
  one straight column).
- **A welcome screen.** When nothing is playing, Music shows a CD with a rainbow shine instead of an empty
  cover, and **Go to Library** in place of the title. Tap it to pick something.
- **Hide the clock with a hold.** Hold the top of Music to show or hide the clock, weather and status icons
  (same as Disco Options › Music Screen › Show Clock).
- **Tag a whole album.** Hold an album in the Library and choose **Tags** to look up tags for every track.
- **Bitrate in Song Info**, next to the file size (the file's average for FLAC and other variable formats).

## Changed

- **Settings, reorganised.** Display, Disco Options and System are split into short pages, so you scroll far
  less: Display › Theme, Text & Layout, Standby, Online & Extras; Disco Options › Music Screen, Colours,
  Layout; System › Power, Date & Time, Library, Controls, Maintenance, About.
- **Disco hold menus** (songs, albums, files, folders) use glass buttons and accent icons over the cover.
- **Battery:** "Since full charge" now starts at 95% (the Disc seldom shows 100%), or 80% with Charging Limit.
- About shows **Disco! 1.3.0**. The old upstream version line is gone from About and Device Info.

## Fixed

- New settings could fail with "Couldn't save settings" on a well-used Disc: the settings store was full.
  It now holds twice as many.

## Install

Flash `payload/mq_ui` with the installer, or sign `mq_ui` from this release with your own keys and install it
from the SD card. The [update guide](../UPDATING.md) has every step. A UI update doesn't reinstall Album
Roulette.
