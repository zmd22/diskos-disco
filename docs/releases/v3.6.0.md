# Disco! 3.6.0

Lyrics that follow the song, a new game and two fixes.

## Progressive lyrics

In **Lyrics** and **Immersive**, lines now glide into place and the words light up as they're sung.

- Lyrics with word timestamps (Enhanced LRC) are followed word by word, exactly.
- Lyrics timed only by line get an estimated word sweep (**Estimate word timing**). It's an approximation and may
  run ahead of or behind the singer.
- **Tap a timed line** to jump to that part of the song.
- Both settings are under **Settings › Display › Text & layout** and are On by default. With **Progressive lyrics**
  Off, whole lines highlight as before.

By @Grippy98 (#29).

## Cat Dash

A small endless runner, built in: a pixel cat jumps boxes and puddles and ducks under birds. Tap to jump, swipe up
for a long jump, swipe down to duck. It speeds up the longer you run, night falls now and then, and your best score
is kept. Find it in **Apps** and **Shortcuts**. It follows every theme, light or dark.

By @ericklv (#26).

## Fixes

- **Zune stuck in Light:** Outdoor mode turned on under another theme no longer keeps Zune in Light, where the Outdoor
  switch is hidden. The setting is kept for the themes that show it. At startup the clock is now read before the
  Auto appearance picks day or night. By @ericklv (#27).
- **Disc Shelf SMB on older cards:** downloads now finish on older FAT/exFAT SD cards that can't move a finished file
  into place. Disc Shelf copies it into a new file instead and never overwrites an existing one; that step needs room
  for a second copy of the file. Errors now name the step that failed. By @Grippy98 (#28).
