# Disco! 3.2.1

A small follow-up to 3.2.0.

## Changed

- **Wheel lists** move more smoothly: the row in the middle grows and the others shrink continuously as you scroll, the rows close up round the middle one and follow the round edge, and the Library's main lists show their size ("80 songs") in the space above the first row.
- **Disco's buttons** have a little depth — a lighter top, a fine light edge and a soft shadow — and flatten when pressed.

## Fixed

- **Queue in Wheel mode:** its list moved over the Play, Shuffle and Clear buttons, which stopped responding, and hid "Nothing queued". The Queue (and lists that start below a status, like Bluetooth and Wi-Fi) now keep their usual motion.
- **Idle power-off on the charger:** this Disc only reports a charger while it is actually charging, so a full battery (or one held at 80 % by Charging optimization) looked unplugged and the Disc could switch itself off on the charger. diskOS now remembers the charger until the battery level drops; a USB connection to a computer counts too.
- **Holding the power button** to switch off: the player's own power-off does not save anything first. diskOS now saves your listening stats and battery history and flushes the card while you hold the button.

## Testing status

The changes were built and checked on the host renderer; the power and charger behaviour cannot be simulated there and is confirmed on the Disc after release. Best-effort hobby release, provided as-is. Back up first, use your own signing keys for an SD update and follow [Updating](../UPDATING.md).
