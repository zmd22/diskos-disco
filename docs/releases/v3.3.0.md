# Disco! 3.3.0

**Braun 2.0** — a new light and dark theme in the spirit of Dieter Rams. Pick it in Settings › Display › Theme.

- **Home:** a large wall clock with date, weather, battery and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth on the face.
- **Music:** artwork beside or above the title, a radio-dial seek scale you can scrub, and an optional gesture zone. Choose in Braun 2.0 settings › Music screen.
- **Quick Settings:** eight keys between battery and brightness scales. Working mode, Shortcuts (six slots), Weather and the track menus use the same keys.
- **Volume:** Braun's dial with a high/low gain switch.
- **Everywhere:** shaded keys with orange icons, Braun sliders on the EQ, and support for Font Size.

**Fixed:** the colon in Braun's large times; the A–Z wheel's large letter in Braun.
