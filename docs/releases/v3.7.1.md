# Disco! 3.7.1

A fix for 3.7.0. Please update.

## Gestures stopped working after an Immersive shortcut

In 3.7.0, opening the Queue, Up Next, Search or Quick Settings from Immersive's new shortcuts left Immersive open
underneath, and the interface kept treating it as the owner of every touch. From then on the Back swipe, the Quick
Settings pull-down and the Home swipe did nothing, on every screen and in every theme, until Immersive was opened and
closed again (a restart also cleared it).

3.7.1 gives Immersive the gestures only while it is the screen you're on, and closes it as soon as Back can no longer
return to it, freeing its memory. The shortcuts themselves work as before.

Sorry for the trouble, and thank you for the quick report.

([guide](../UPDATING.md)); no reflash.
