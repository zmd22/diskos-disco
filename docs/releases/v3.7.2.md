# Disco! 3.7.2

Two community fixes and better cover art for Opus files.

## The screensaver shows what the Disc is doing

The screensaver used to keep showing the last song played from the card, even after you switched to USB DAC, BT DAC,
USB storage or AirPlay. It now shows the mode instead, in every theme:

- **USB DAC:** "USB DAC" and the sample rate the computer sends.
- **BT DAC:** "BT" and the codec (e.g. "BT AAC", "BT LDAC"), plus the phone's song and its progress. To get the song,
  Disco opens the Bluetooth remote-control link to the phone once per connection; FiiO's firmware never opened it.
- **AirPlay:** "AirPlay" and the song.
- **USB storage:** "USB storage".

Thanks to @powahbeng (#36).

## No more black screen after a power press

If the screen had turned off on its own (Screen Off) and you pressed power, the Disc could stay black and ignore touch
until a restart. Fixed. Thanks to @ericklv (#37).

## Cover art in more Opus and Ogg files

3.7.0 added embedded covers for Opus and Ogg Vorbis. 3.7.2 also reads covers from files written by tools that wrap the
picture data over several lines or leave out its end padding, and from Opus/Ogg files with an ID3 tag in front.

If your Opus covers only appeared with Online Album Art on, you're on a version older than 3.7.0: this update fixes it.

Binary record: [RELEASE_3.7.2.md](../RELEASE_3.7.2.md). Update from the SD card as usual ([guide](../UPDATING.md));
no reflash.
