# Disco! 3.7.0

Shortcuts from Immersive, a Back swipe in Search everywhere, the real sample rate in USB DAC mode and cover art for
Ogg files.

## Immersive shortcuts

Tap the artwork to bring up Immersive's controls. Two small arrows now sit at the edges:

- **Bottom:** tap it, or pull up from the bottom edge, for the **Queue**, or **Up Next** when nothing is queued.
- **Right:** tap it, or pull in from the right edge, for **Search**.
- **Top:** pull down from the top edge for **Quick Settings**, straight away, without the now-playing Shade.

Each opens your theme's own screen, and Back brings you straight back to Immersive. The shortcuts only work while the
controls are showing: with them hidden, the edges belong to the cover and lyrics, and a drag around the rim stays a
Circular control seek or volume change. **lyrics on / off** sits a little higher to make room.

## Back in Search

The keyboard that opens over Search (Zune opens Search straight into it) now takes the usual Back swipe from the side
edge, the same as its **cancel**. Search can be left with a Back swipe in every theme, whether it was opened from the
menu or from Immersive.

## USB DAC sample rate

In USB DAC mode the screen showed the rate of the last local song, not the rate the computer was sending; setting
96 kHz on a Mac still read 48 kHz. Audio was always right; now the label is too. When the computer isn't streaming,
the line stays blank. By @powahbeng (#34).

## Ogg cover art

Covers embedded in Ogg Opus and Ogg Vorbis files (`METADATA_BLOCK_PICTURE` / `COVERART`) now show wherever covers
appear, with the same size and memory limits as the other formats.
By @mean-ui-thread (#35).
